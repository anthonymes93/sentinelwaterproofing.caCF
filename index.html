How do interior crack injections work?

We inject a polyurethane resin directly into foundation cracks from the inside. It expands, fills the void, and creates a flexible, waterproof seal that stops water from entering your basement.

Do you need to excavate my foundation?

No. All of our work is done from inside your basement, which means no digging, no landscaping damage, and minimal disruption to your property.

How long does the repair last?

When properly installed, polyurethane injections create a long-lasting waterproof seal designed to move with your foundation and prevent future leaks.

Can you fix active leaks or only dry cracks?

We can repair both active leaking cracks and dormant ones. Our system is designed to stop water even under pressure.

What areas do you service?

We service London, Middlesex, Strathroy-Caradoc, Woodstock, Ingersoll, St. Thomas, Kitchener-Waterloo, and Sarnia.
